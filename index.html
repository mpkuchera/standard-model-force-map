Where is gravity? Gravity is not part of the Standard Model. It acts on everything that has energy, and its proposed carrier, the graviton, has never been detected.

Chart design follows Standard Model of Elementary Particles by Cush on Wikimedia Commons (public domain, CC0), including its masses, colors and force outlines. The Higgs interaction appears beside the three forces because it has its own boson, though it is not usually counted as a fundamental force.
